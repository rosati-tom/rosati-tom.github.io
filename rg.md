## Reading group on the Renormalisation Group

A reading group on rigorous renormalisation group methods, organised with Benoit Dagallier, in the Academic year 2026/2027.

It starts on **Friday 6 November 2026** and meets weekly for 1 to 1.5 hours. Room and time TBA. We decide each week what to cover next. Everyone is welcome: get in touch if you would like to join.

**Main reference:** R. Bauerschmidt, D. Brydges and G. Slade, [*Introduction to a Renormalisation Group Method*](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-981-32-9593-3), Springer (2019). Freely available from [Slade's webpage](https://www.math.ubc.ca/~slade/bauerschmidt_brydges_slade_book_180629.pdf).
The book treats the hierarchical φ⁴₄ model at the critical point: Part 1 is a general introduction, Part 2 sets up the RG map and carries out the perturbative computations, and Part 3 controls the non-perturbative remainder.

**Other entry points:**
- L. Ferdinand and S. Gabriel, [a Poisson problem for a hierarchical Anderson Hamiltonian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17177).
- [arXiv:2107.01878](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.01878), an RG analysis for fermionic models, where many estimates simplify.
